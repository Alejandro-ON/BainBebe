Children's nursing care, with time to explain first.

This is a small nursing practice for children from birth to fourteen. Vaccinations, wound care, blood draws and growth checks, done at the pace the child needs rather than the pace of a waiting list.

Why families come here

Most of our families arrive after a visit somewhere else that went badly. A frightened child is not an awkward patient, it is a sign nobody explained things. So we build the appointment around that.

Appointments that are not rushed

Every slot lasts at least twenty minutes, so there is room to stop, breathe and start again.

You can ask for the nurse your child already knows. Continuity does most of the work for us.

We schedule in fixed slots, so nobody spends forty minutes getting more nervous in a corridor.

How a visit works

Four steps, from the first message to the follow up call.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send the booking form at the bottom of this page or call us. We answer the same working day.

  2. We prepare the room before you arrive

    Anaesthetic cream, the right needle size and, when it helps, a short illustrated story about what is coming.

  3. The appointment itself

    A parent stays in the room the whole time. The child decides which arm, and we never hold a child down by surprise.

  4. We check in afterwards

    A short call the next day for vaccinations and wound care, and a printed summary for your paediatrician.

What we do

Vaccinations
The official schedule plus any vaccine you bring from the pharmacy. We use cold spray, sweet solution for infants and guided distraction for older children, then fifteen minutes of observation before you leave.

Common questions

Do I need a referral?
Only for blood draws, where we need the requesting form. Everything else you can book directly.
Can both parents come in?
Yes, and siblings too if that helps. The room fits four adults comfortably.
What if my child refuses on the day?
We stop. You can rebook within the week at no extra cost, and we will talk through what to try differently.
How do you handle my data?
The booking form sends your message to our inbox and nowhere else. Clinical notes stay on an encrypted local system and are never shared without written consent.
